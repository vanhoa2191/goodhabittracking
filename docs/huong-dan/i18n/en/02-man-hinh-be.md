Your child opens this screen with a [code or QR code](05-gia-dinh-va-cai-dat.md#ghep-thiet-bi), or you can select "Back to your child's screen" from the parent account. Your child only sees their own data.

<a id="bon-tab"></a>
## Four tabs

| Tab | Content |
|---|---|
| **Tasks** | Today's tasks, grouped by time of day; the morning letter, journal, and Dream City are here |
| **Leaderboard** | Family, group, and public leaderboards |
| **Redeem rewards** | Rewards store, reward goals, and requested rewards |
| **Badges** | Badges that have been unlocked and those still locked |

The top of the page shows a greeting, mascot, level, star count, streak, and today's progress.

<a id="nhiem-vu"></a>
## Daily tasks

- Tasks are grouped into **Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Any time** according to the schedule you set when you [create a task](04-thiet-ke-thoi-quen.md#quan-ly-viec).
- Each task appears only on the days it repeats (every day, weekdays, weekends, or selected days).
- The "Previous day" and "Next day" buttons let your child view and mark days near today; the server accepts only dates from **two days before through one day after** today.
- When all tasks are complete, the message "You completed all of today's tasks!" appears. If there are no tasks today: "Enjoy your day!".
- If the family is [taking a break](#tam-nghi-be), progress reminders and the streak are hidden.

<a id="hoan-thanh"></a>
## Complete, undo, do later

- **Complete**: tap the circle or **swipe right**. The card changes state immediately, "+N" stars float up, the device gives a gentle vibration, and a sound plays when the save succeeds. If saving fails, the card returns to its previous state and shows "Could not save. Please try again." with a short code in parentheses for you to share with support.
- **Tasks requiring parent approval**: after your child marks a task complete, the card changes to "Waiting for approval" ("You did it! Now a parent will check."), and **no stars are added** until you [approve it](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#duyet).
- **Undo**: tap a completed task again to unmark it. If its stars have already been used to redeem a reward, the app does not allow undoing it, so the balance cannot go below zero.
- **Do later**: **swipe left** or tap "Do later" to move the task to "Later today"; a postponed task does not count as missed. Tap "Do now" to bring it back. Completing a postponed task automatically removes its postponed status.
- From age 15, after completing a habit your child can choose "How did you do it this time?" (on their own, with a reminder, or together with someone else), or skip it; see [recording how your child did it](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#muc-ho-tro). For habits that have become routine, your child sees verbal recognition instead of only a star reward.

<a id="chi-tiet"></a>
### Details, timer, read aloud

<a id="dem-gio"></a>
- **Task details**: tap a task to read its "Meaning" and "How to do it" (and the duration, if any) before marking it complete.
- **Timer**: tasks with a time limit (for example, brushing teeth for 2 minutes) have **Start**, **Pause**, **Reset**, and **Complete time** buttons.
<a id="doc-to"></a>
- **Read aloud**: the speaker button reads the task content using the device's voice, if supported.
- **Sound**: use the button to turn sound on or off; from 20:00 until before 07:00, the volume is reduced by half.
- **Text size and font**: adjust them in the "Text and font size settings" window ([interface settings](05-gia-dinh-va-cai-dat.md#giao-dien)).

<a id="sao-cap-chuoi"></a>
## Stars, levels, and streaks

- **Stars** are reward points. Each task adds the number of stars you set. Stars can be spent (on rewards or building the city), while **total stars earned** never decrease.
- **Levels** increase with every 100 stars earned: fewer than 100 is Level 1, 100 or more is Level 2, and so on.
- **Streaks** (the flame) count consecutive days with confirmed completed tasks. If your child has not done anything today, yesterday's streak is kept (the flame switches to "resting") until the end of the day; leaving another day empty resets the streak to 0 and starts the count again. Family [break days](#tam-nghi-be) do not break the streak and do not add to it.
- **League rank** is based on total stars earned: Bronze, Silver (70+), Gold (150+), Diamond (300+), shown on the [leaderboard](#bang-xep-hang).

Stars and streaks are only for motivation. They are not used to judge character or compare children ([principles](06-khoa-hoc-thoi-quen.md#nguyen-tac)).

<a id="huy-hieu"></a>
## Badges

The **Badges** tab shows unlocked and locked badges, with progress. There are two groups:

- **Five basic badges**: Green starter (first task), 3-day flame, 7-day warrior, Good Deeds Superhero (20 tasks), 100-star crown.
- **16 portrait badges**: each portrait unlocks when your child completes enough tasks from the [habit framework](06-khoa-hoc-thoi-quen.md#chan-dung) linked to that portrait; the 16th portrait, the peak, unlocks when your child has kept all the others.

Unlocking a new badge shows a celebration screen. You can also share a family milestone without your child's name or photo ([share a milestone](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#thong-ke)).

<a id="qua"></a>
## Rewards

The **Redeem rewards** tab is where your child spends stars. You create rewards in the [rewards store](04-thiet-ke-thoi-quen.md#kho-qua).

1. **Reward goal**: your child taps "Set as goal" on an item to track how many stars are still needed. The goal is saved for your child under "Dream reward goals". This is not a reward request yet.
2. **Request a reward**: your child taps "Redeem this reward". Stars are **deducted immediately**, and the request is sent to you ("Reward redemption request sent to your parents!"). If there are not enough stars, "Not enough stars to redeem" appears; an out-of-stock reward cannot be redeemed.
3. **Parent approval**: the request appears in [Today](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#duyet). You **approve** it and then **give the reward**; if you **reject** it, the stars are returned to your child.
4. Your child's **rewards store** shows the status of each item: Waiting for approval, Approved, Reward given.

<a id="bang-xep-hang"></a>
## Leaderboard

There are three scopes and three periods:

| Scope | Who can see it | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Family** | Children in the household | None |
| **Group / Clan** | Members of a group | Your child created or joined a group |
| **Global** | Children whose parents allow sharing | The family account is connected; you [turn on sharing](05-gia-dinh-va-cai-dat.md#rieng-tu) and choose the child; **off** by default |

Periods: Today, This week, This month. The ranking score is **the stars earned during the period**; spending stars on rewards does not change the ranking.

- **Groups**: create a group (name, group reward, and a weekly star goal for the whole group; the system issues a **6-character invite code**) or enter an invite code to join. Group members can send encouragement with "High five".
- The public board shows only a **nickname** (or "Bé Siêu Nhân" if none is set), avatar, points for the period, streak, and rank; it does not show a real name, age, or code ([privacy](09-bao-mat-va-rieng-tu.md#bxh-cong-khai)).
- When the family is taking a break, the leaderboard tab is hidden from your child's screen.

<a id="linh-vat"></a>
## Mascots and colors

There are six mascots: **Leo** (lion, practicing courage through small tasks), **Bunny** (rabbit, caring about others), **Panda** (panda, staying calm to see more clearly), **Fox** (fox, curious about new things), **Turtle** (turtle, patiently moving alongside your child), and **Bee** (bee, happy when the whole family helps one another). Your child can change their mascot **at most once every 7 days**; the screen shows when it can be changed again. The **main color** can be changed at any time. You can also choose a mascot when creating a [profile](05-gia-dinh-va-cai-dat.md#ho-so).

<a id="thu-buoi-sang"></a>
## Morning letter

From **07:00 in the device's time zone**, your child receives a new letter each day from their mascot (an introduction, a gentle message, and a closing). Your child taps "I have read it"; the letter remains available to read again during the day, and the read status syncs to the child's other devices. This feature is **on**.

<a id="nhat-ky"></a>
## One-sentence journal

In the "One thing I want to remember" card, your child writes **one sentence** (up to 280 characters, without line breaks) about what they learned, did well, or want to try again tomorrow, then taps "Save journal". Only your child and parents can see it; the content is **not used for measurement**. You can reread it and export a CSV in [Statistics](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#thong-ke). This feature is **on**.

<a id="thanh-pho"></a>
## Dream City

Your child uses stars to build four structures: **Green Garden** (30 stars), **Library** (60), **Connection Bridge** (90), and **Observatory** (120). Built structures remain forever. Total stars earned are not deducted, so progress and achieved levels are never lost. If there are not enough stars, "Not enough points" appears. This feature is **on**.

<a id="giao-dien-tuoi"></a>
## Age-based interface

Your child's screen adapts automatically to three age bands: **3–8**, **9–12**, and **13 and up**. The youngest band has larger buttons and a more prominent mascot; older bands are more streamlined. From age 13, the interface uses "points" instead of "stars" and does not call your child "little one". Children under 3 are guided by their parents, so the screen stays unchanged.

- The first time the screen changes, your child can choose "Keep the old look" or "Use the new look", then choose the "Lean" or "With a companion" style.
- You can **pin** an age band for your child or keep the old look when [editing your child's profile](05-gia-dinh-va-cai-dat.md#ho-so); your choice takes priority over the choice on the child's device.

<a id="tam-nghi-be"></a>
## Family pause

When you tap [Take a break](05-gia-dinh-va-cai-dat.md#tam-nghi), your child's screen shows "Our family is taking a break today. You can still do a good habit whenever you want." Your child can still do tasks; stars, rewards, and the streak are not deleted. Progress reminders and the leaderboard are hidden.
