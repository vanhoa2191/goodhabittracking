The **Design** area (`Design`) has three sections: Habits, Age Roadmaps, and Rewards. A plan or active trial is required ([7](07-goi-va-thanh-toan.md)).

<a id="quan-ly-viec"></a>
## Managing habits

`Design → Habits` has three views:

| View | Used for |
|---|---|
| **In use** | See assigned habits, filter by child or "All children", view completions in the past 7 days, edit, delete, and set cues |
| **Library** | Choose more from the template library ([library](#thu-vien)) |
| **Programs** | Start a group of habits to build step by step ([programs](#chuong-trinh)); shown only in Vietnamese |

Each habit is a card for **one child** or **the whole family** ("Assign to"). A paused habit remains saved but does not appear on the [child screen](02-man-hinh-be.md#nhiem-vu).

<a id="tao-viec"></a>
## Creating or editing a habit

The "Create New Habit" button opens a form:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Habit title | For example, "Wash hands before eating" |
| Description and purpose | Shown for the child to read in the "Why it matters" section of [task details](02-man-hinh-be.md#chi-tiet) |
| Icon | A small image for the habit |
| Development category | Healthy dining, good deeds, self-care, learning, health, virtues, mindset… |
| Reward stars | Stars the child receives when the habit is complete |
| Repeat | Every day, Mon–Fri, Sat & Sun, or custom days |
| Time of day | Morning, afternoon, evening, or anytime |
| Timer countdown | Number of minutes for the [timer](02-man-hinh-be.md#dem-gio); 0 means no countdown |
| Requires parent approval | When enabled, the child does **not** receive stars after marking it complete until a parent [approves it](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#duyet) |
| Assign to | All children or one child |

After applying a template from the library or a roadmap, parents can still adjust the time and stars, add habits, or delete habits to fit the family's routine. Each habit records completion **only once per child per day** (double-tapping does not count twice).

<a id="thu-vien"></a>
## Library

The library depends on the language in use:

- **Vietnamese**: [47-habit framework for ages 0–18](#khung-47).
- **Other languages**: "Recommended habits library for children" with eight groups of suggestions (healthy dining and mealtimes, good deeds and giving, virtues, mindset, harmonious personality, wisdom and learning, capacity and independence, physical vitality), with names and descriptions translated into that language. Choose "Add to the task list" to add one to the schedule.

In addition to these two libraries, parents can load an **age-based habit pack** (six starter habits) from [Children](05-gia-dinh-va-cai-dat.md#ho-so), or apply an [age roadmap](#lo-trinh).

<a id="khung-47"></a>
## 47-habit framework for ages 0–18

The Vietnamese library contains the **habit framework** ([framework explanation in 6](06-khoa-hoc-thoi-quen.md#khung)). How to use it:

1. Choose a **stage of development** (5 stages) and an **area of the habit**: Inner life, Health, Relationships, Learning, Money, or "All".
2. Each card shows a suggested age range, **signs of progress**, the adult's part, how the child can self-track, and the **"See how to do it and how to support"** button. Age is only a guide; adjust it to your child's actual abilities.
3. Tap **"Add to the task list"**; the card changes to "Added to the family". Habits added from the framework are **shared with the whole family**. To assign one to a specific child, edit "Assign to" ([create or edit a habit](#tao-viec)) or use a [program](#chuong-trinh) or [age roadmap](#lo-trinh), which lets you choose the child when applying it. The card also shows the number of completions in the past 7 days.

Habits added from the framework keep a link to the original habit (framework code), so when a child completes one, the app records progress for the [16 portrait badges](02-man-hinh-be.md#huy-hieu) and for [programs](#chuong-trinh). Health content is for reference only and does not replace professional advice. Framework translations are available in English, Korean, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Chinese, and Japanese; languages without a translation use the English version.

<a id="chuong-trinh"></a>
## Programs and cues

**Adaptive habit programs** (currently enabled) help build habits step by step instead of assigning many habits at once.

**"If… then…" cues**: each habit can have a plan in the child's own words, such as "After brushing my teeth, I read one page."

- The **Cue** button on an in-use habit card opens a form: cue type (**after something familiar** or **at a fixed time**), the plan in the child's words, time of day, where to do it (optional), a plan for weekends or busy days (optional), and which child it is for. A card with a cue set shows "Cue set".
- Five cue templates are available: after waking up, after breakfast, after coming home from school, after dinner, and after brushing teeth at night.

**Starting a program** (Programs view, Vietnamese only): for each child, the app suggests a group of habits and guides you through three steps.

1. **Choose the habits to start with**: start with only a few, usually one or two, up to the [age-based recommended limit](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#hom-nay). Selecting more than the limit shows a warning, but you can continue.
2. **Set a cue** for each habit using a template or your own words.
3. **Confirm**: add the habits for the child and save the cues. When finished, you return to the "In use" view.

Once cues are set, the app tracks the **phase** of each habit (setting the cue, building the routine, easing off support, now a routine), records [how the child did it](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#muc-ho-tro), and provides [adjustment suggestions](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#tien-trien) in Today. The detailed logic is in [6](06-khoa-hoc-thoi-quen.md#logic).

<a id="lo-trinh"></a>
## Age roadmaps

`Design → Age Roadmaps` replaces the fixed weekly or monthly roadmaps used previously because habit research does not provide one universal number of days.

- **5 age stages**: 0–3 "Safety and senses", 3–6 "Exploration and willpower", 6–12 "Diligence and skills", 12–15 "Identity and emotions", and 15–18 "Direction and responsibility". Each stage suggests the adult's role: model, do it together, monitor, support, or advise.
- Each stage lasts about **12 weeks** and has **3 steps**; **each step adds only ONE new habit** while keeping previous habits. The week marker is a point to review, **not a deadline**: each family stays on a step for as long as suits the child.
- The app selects a stage based on the selected child's age; you can switch to another stage. Each step shows progress (how many habits have been assigned and practiced) and the current step.
- Tap a step to **view details**, choose "Apply to which child?", then **Confirm and apply**. Habits are added to the child's daily schedule; habits already assigned are not duplicated.

In Vietnamese, the tab also reminds parents that the roadmap only suggests the **order for adding habits**, while [programs](#chuong-trinh) follow each child's own pace.

<a id="kho-qua"></a>
## Rewards store

`Design → Rewards` is where parents create rewards that children can redeem with stars ([how children redeem rewards](02-man-hinh-be.md#qua)).

- **Create a reward**: reward name, number of stars required (from 5 stars), description or conditions. Rewards created here have no limit on the number of redemptions. They can be edited or deleted.
- **Ideas for meaningful rewards**: the template library has two types, **experiences** (time together, experiences, and the right to choose) and **purposeful things** (supporting hobbies, movement, learning, and the spirit of giving), with suggested star amounts. Choose "Add to rewards".
- Children's requests appear under [Today → Approvals](03-hom-nay-va-duyet-viec.md#duyet).

The app recommends prioritizing time together and experiences over things ([recognition principles](06-khoa-hoc-thoi-quen.md#nguyen-tac)).
