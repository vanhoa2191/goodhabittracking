# KidHabit Sales Page Reference Analysis

## Summary

Independent UI/UX agent analysis found the reference stronger than the current homepage in conversion structure and visible product proof. Recommendation: adapt its decision journey, not its visual identity or unsupported claims.

## Reusable Patterns

| Priority | Pattern | KidHabit adaptation | Reference lines |

## Do Not Copy

- Prototype colors, external Baloo/Nunito fonts, heavy shadows, pervasive pills, or custom star mascot.
- Placeholder product interfaces, family photograph, dead links, toast-only buttons, or fixed Zalo control.
- Testimonials, family counts, rankings, or child-development outcomes without consented evidence.
- JavaScript-first reveal behavior that leaves content hidden when enhancement fails.
- 11px labels, 30px controls, ambiguous “Đánh dấu” buttons, or broken mobile-menu ARIA state.
- The full 14,416px mobile page; keep secondary framework and roadmap detail collapsed.

## Recommended Homepage Order

1. Hero and real product proof.
2. Verified assurance strip.
3. Parent problem, KidHabit mechanism, observable outcome.
4. Three product surfaces.
5. Three-step setup.
6. Collapsed framework and roadmap.
7. Pricing.
8. Safety and parent controls.
9. FAQ.
10. Final CTA and real footer links.
