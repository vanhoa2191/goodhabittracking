# Daily wisdom

Data for the daily popup: one saying a day and one "Ngày này năm xưa" fact.

- `source/*.txt` – curated sayings (`kind|months|author|text`). `tn` tục ngữ, `cd` ca dao, `dn` danh ngôn (always with an
  author), `ch` câu nói hay (modern, unattributed). Sayings that name a month or season carry a month list and are only
  shown in those months (`tests/unit/daily-wisdom.test.ts` enforces this). Build with `node scripts/build-daily-wisdom.mjs`.
- `quotes-vi.json` – generated by the script above; do not edit by hand.
- `history-vi.json` – one event per calendar day (`MM-DD`). Taken from the "Sự kiện" and "Sinh" sections of the Vietnamese
  Wikipedia day pages (https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_tháng_1 …), chosen for children: no wars, deaths or politics. Where the
  page offered nothing suitable, a well-known dated fact replaced it.

## Sources and licence

Proverbs and folk songs are traditional. Many lines were collected from Wikiquote (vi) pages "Ca dao tục ngữ Việt Nam",
"Tục ngữ Việt Nam" and the pages of the people quoted; history facts come from Vietnamese Wikipedia. Both are CC BY-SA 4.0,
so the popup shows a source line.
